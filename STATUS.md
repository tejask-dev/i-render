## What works on this Mac

- **From a drawing to a model.** The local vision model interpreted three different reference drawings. The workbench exported named GLB parts and millimetre STL geometry. Snack Scout produced seven selectable parts. Its fresh sketch and drawer reads took 5.75 and 3.28 seconds. The interview supplies the target size. Front-view geometry leaves depth, hidden surfaces and joints estimated.
- **From a drawer to a plan.** The combined reference image produced boxes for both objects. The saved plan allocates one motor, two wheels and one LED as candidates. The six remaining units appear in the sourcing list. SQLite retained the brief, model, part counts, photos and plan after reopening. In a harder check, vision located five objects in each of three reference layouts. A real electronics-pile photo exposed a power-supply grille mistaken for a fan. Photo-identified fans and optical drives now require a detached, physically verified part before manual matching. [Images and findings](eval/inputs/drawer-stress/README.md) The offline fallback is that specific saved project link. A fresh run with a different brief misses the saved recognition and needs the vision model again. The first photo after about 15 idle minutes also waits for the model to reload.
- **Voice and presentation.** A recorded browser test passed through local Whisper into the size field. The 64-second recording is an automated run of the real interface, with no person at the keyboard. The camera buttons captured a generated test frame and waited for a separate analysis click. Desktop and phone-size layouts passed model selection, save and reopen at 2× display density. On-device dictation is preferred. The optional browser speech service and Gemini routes can send data off the Mac and are labelled in the interface. A physical microphone, room noise, actual donor yield and printed fit remain on-site checks.
- **Prize evidence.** Overall and Most Adventurous fit the demonstrated build. Brian can revisit PCBWay after physical assembly. The optional Gemini adapter has a mock-verified request path. The live rehearsal used local Qwen and SQLite. [Sponsor criteria and awards](event-guide.md)
