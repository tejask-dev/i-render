## Where the team needs to be

Hack Western is Western University’s annual student-run, in-person hackathon. This edition runs for 36 hours at the Ronald D. Schmeichel Building, 1400 Western Road, London, Ontario. The event theme is discovery, and teams can choose their own problem. Organizers provide meals, workshops and mentorship. The FAQ describes travel reimbursement as partial, contingent on remaining funds and project submission. ## What the team can enter

One project per team, one team per participant, up to four accepted and registered members. Detailed rules allow students and graduates from the preceding year and exclude organizers, judges, sponsors, volunteers and others with privileged roles. Devpost also displays its age-of-majority and college-student eligibility labels. The event FAQ specifically excludes high-school applicants. [Current rules](https://hack-western-13.devpost.com/rules) · [Registration labels](https://hack-western-13.devpost.com/) · [Organizer FAQ](https://www.hackwestern.com/)

Ideas can predate the event. Build the project during the hacking window. Public libraries, frameworks and public code are allowed. Previous hackathon submissions and cross-submission to multiple hackathons are disallowed. Hardware teams can prepare structural pieces in advance and begin assembly during hacking. The event links its conduct policy, which covers on-site and online interactions. [Rules](https://hack-western-13.devpost.com/rules) · [Code of conduct](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md)

MLH’s standard guidance allows AI assistance, including code generation, and asks teams to list the tools and explain their use. Record what the teammates designed, built, integrated and verified, and what Codex and Claude supplied. MLH describes these as standard rules that organizers can adapt. Use HW13’s own stop time and demo format for this event. [MLH policy on AI and code generation](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/standard-hackathon-rules.md#ai-tools-and-code-gen)

## What judges will see

Overall judges evaluate originality, technical difficulty, whether the project works, and the quality of its design. For a three-minute demo, start with a real user need, perform the core action live, show the result and explain one engineering decision. Keep enough time to reset before the next judge. Awards per member are an 11-inch iPad for first, AirPods Pro 3 for second and Fitbit Air for third. The organizer’s discovery award offers an Arc’teryx toque. [Judging and prizes](https://hack-western-13.devpost.com/) · [Live demo format](https://hack-western-13.devpost.com/rules)
